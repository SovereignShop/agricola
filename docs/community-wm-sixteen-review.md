# Original WM / G2 batch sixteen

The ten actual English WM prints were downloaded from the PlayAgricola archive, visually checked for identity, edition, cost, prerequisites, and complete printed rules, and stored under `resources/public/art/agricola/cards/`. Every file is registered in `card_art.cljs` and the artwork manifest with its source URL and SHA256. Rules were cross-checked against the [unofficial compendium](https://www.reedstonefood.com/agricola/compendium). The print remains authoritative where summaries omit detail.

| Card test table | Print | Interaction review and rules |
|---|---|---|
| Branch Collector | [WM007](http://playagricola.com/Agricola/Cards/Cards/occx1BranchCollector_859_10.jpg) | Existing direct room/improvement controls. Automatic wood is received before payment, once per room or renovation/improvement, only if clay or stone is actually paid after replacement/discounts. Free construction gives no wood. |
| Contractor | [WM011](http://playagricola.com/Agricola/Cards/Cards/occx1Contractor_2120_8.jpg) | Pictured resource-to-food payment options; direct planned-room selection supports a different replaced resource for each room. Renovation and major improvement each allow one replacement. |
| Crop Rotator | [WM014](http://playagricola.com/Agricola/Cards/Cards/occx1CropRotator_1900_12.jpg) | Show the opposite crop and the exact newly emptied field, then confirm or decline. On play removes one crop from every owned planted field without triggering harvest income. Reactive removals include harvest and card effects. Opponent fields never rotate; card fields retain crop restrictions. |
| Forest Farmer | [WM023](http://playagricola.com/Agricola/Cards/Cards/occx1ForestFarmer_2299_6.jpg) | Reuse direct action-board worker relocation: click source Farmland/Cultivation, then a free destination. Three visible arrow markers; wood and a marker spent only on a committed relocation, once each round, before Returning Home. No duplicate movement of a worker. |
| Godfather | [WM025](http://playagricola.com/Agricola/Cards/Cards/occx3Godfather_921_10.jpg) | Automatic grain before taking an action orthogonally adjacent to an occupied Family Growth round card. Shared physical board geometry follows revealed order and supports five-player tables. |
| Animal Nursery | [WM057](http://playagricola.com/Agricola/Cards/Cards/xminorAnimalNursery_665_10.jpg) | Tailored graphical newborn quantities in breeding. Newborns may enter permanent Nursery storage; existing animals cannot. Stored animals are excluded from supply, cooking, breeding, and harvest-income effects, but included in scoring. Nursery capacity makes breeding mandatory. |
| Back Door | [WM061](http://playagricola.com/Agricola/Cards/Cards/xminorBackDoor_2217_8.jpg) | Play through the normal minor-card face, then directly click revealed Family Growth and perform its full action, even occupied. Consumes a real unplaced worker immediately. Must have room and another worker; passes to the next player after resolution. |
| Donkey | [WM074](http://playagricola.com/Agricola/Cards/Cards/xminorDonkey_138_9.jpg) | Click any cell in an actual fenced pasture; a clay marker appears there. Reopen the card ability to move it. Its pasture cannot house animals. Existing direct extra-plow flow adds one field to Farmland/Cultivation, including combinations with another plow. |
| Fish Hook | [WM080](http://playagricola.com/Agricola/Cards/Cards/xminorFishHook_630_4.jpg) | Optional pictured food transfer from the actual Fishing pile after an adjacent action. No Fishing action is taken and no Fishing-trigger card fires. Empty pile gives no offer. |
| Ram | [WM102](http://playagricola.com/Agricola/Cards/Cards/xminorRam_2141_8.jpg) | Pictured one-food payment or discard during feeding, with no unrelated farm. Virtual sheep counts for breeding/scoring only. Additional births in rounds 6/8/10/12 require housing or Nursery storage; graphical destination choices cannot decline a legal birth. |

Prepared positions use native card play and native triggering actions; none inject a pending effect queue. Each is a fresh creator-controlled table under **Card test tables**, and Next card advances through this batch.

Verification: native tests cover legal/illegal payments, per-room replacements, crop alternation and restricted card fields, board adjacency, marker movement, extra plowing, stored newborns, Ram feeding/breeding/scoring distinctions, Back Door occupied growth and worker consumption, Forest Farmer marker/payment semantics, optional declines, store reload, and recorded playback. The full existing prepared-table registration audit also passes with these additions. `test/browser_community_wm_sixteen.py` covers web, desktop Activity and mobile Activity, direct board/farm choices, graphical payment/feeding choices, dialog width and no-dropdown checks. Browser execution is deferred to the parent's combined advanced build; this document does not claim that unrun check passed.
