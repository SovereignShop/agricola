# Original G-deck batch fifteen

These ten actual English G-deck prints are stored locally and recorded with source URLs and SHA-256 checksums in the artwork manifest. Printed costs and prerequisites take precedence; interactions follow the [Agricola compendium](https://www.reedstonefood.com/agricola/compendium).

| Card test table | Interaction reviewed | Rule details |
|---|---|---|
| Headmaster | Card face and food-payment icons | Stored food is not personal supply. It pays occupation fees and additional printed food; receipt precedes Educator. Four-player scoring divides by three, five-player scoring by four. |
| Mathematician | Click the actual action space | From round six, equal wood/clay piles qualify; stone qualifies only with five players. One resource per round. |
| Miner | Click a marked Stone action space | Day Laborer creates claims for Returning Home. Collect one entire revealed stone pile without placing a worker. |
| Opportunist | Building-resource icons | Only goods taken directly from action spaces count; the last action includes worker relocation. |
| Pilgrim | Resource icons and optional discard quantities | Two food arrive before the occupation fee. The round-start gift may precede scheduled deliveries. Stored personal food counts. |
| Pond Watchman | Click the fisherman, then the destination action space | Relocates the existing worker to a free wood accumulation space for one food; declining spends nothing. |
| Animal Farm | Animal-to-guest exchange | One animal per round buys a guest. The player receives a final opportunity on their next turn after their last family member. Removing the card removes its unplaced guest. |
| Bountiful Harvest | Automatic stock marker and optional food collection | Two harvested resources plus breeding award one stored food. Food is personal supply, automatically funds costs and feeding, and scores while stored. Earlier harvests and Harvest Helper count if the card is played before breeding. |
| Chronicle | Food markers on the major-improvements board | Two wood OR two clay; one occupation. Only majors still available when played receive markers. Each marked major pays its owner once, whoever acquires it. |
| Dunghill | Click planted farm or card fields | Two/three animal types allow one/two distinct stacks. Matching wood/reed crops and underlying stacks qualify. House Goat does not count. |

Native tests exercise the ten prepared tables through the normal command path, optional declines, reloading saves, and recording playback. The browser regression covers desktop web, desktop Discord, and mobile Discord with direct spatial interactions and checks for dropdown-free card resolutions.
