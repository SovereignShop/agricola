# Community batch twelve review ledger

Thirty Original-edition cards: ten each from G, WM/G2, and FR/G3. Every row has a prepared table under **Card test tables**, using the card name shown below. Prints are locally installed and tracked with full SHA-256 checksums in `resources/public/art/agricola/manifest.json`.

The final English prints are the authority for identity, cost, prerequisites, and printed rules. The [Agricola compendium](https://www.reedstonefood.com/agricola/compendium) supplies interaction clarifications. Meat Pie uses its printed cost of two grain or one animal; the compendium’s food wording is not used.
