Agricola artwork

Agricola is designed by Uwe Rosenberg, with illustrations by Klemens Franz, and published by Lookout Games. Eurozone is an unofficial implementation.

The farm and resource/piece illustrations are sourced from the Korean Revised Edition assets in dslov89/agricola, pinned source revision. Files are unmodified; the board and farm bitmaps were extracted from their SVG wrappers. Eurozone adds English interface labels and displays the effects its game engine implements.

These images remain third-party artwork and are not covered by the application source code license. The asset manifest records each source and SHA-256 checksum.

Ninety English Revised Edition base occupation and minor-improvement card scans come from JiaYouChen2003/agricola.tools, pinned source revision. These files are unmodified. Six base cards use text fallbacks. The 48 occupations from the 5–6 Player Expansion have native rules in five-player games and currently use text cards. Matching card scans have not been sourced.

The fourteen round-card fronts and six stage backs use original English edition scans referenced by SNiLD/TabletopSimulatorAgricola, pinned source revision. The two image sheets are unmodified and displayed with CSS sprite positioning. Eurozone overlays Revised Edition action names and applies Revised Edition rules.

The ten major improvements now use the unmodified English card sheet referenced by the same pinned SNiLD repository. Printed costs and effects were checked against the game definitions. The major-improvements supply board also uses an unmodified English scan from the same source, with available cards placed over its ten printed slots. The tabletop uses three unmodified English original-edition board scans from that same source: the basic-action board, rounds 1–7, and rounds 8–14. They retain their original proportions. Eurozone keeps its Revised Edition rules and places the corresponding player-count action choices in the left segment’s card area.
